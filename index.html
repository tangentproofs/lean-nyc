leannyc explained

Lean 4 projects by members of Lean NYC, each read back into plain English: statements, proof outlines, quizzes.

An independent fan index. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NYC Lean or by the projects' authors. Every project belongs to its authors, linked on its card. The explainers are unofficial and AI-assisted (written by Claude, scaffolded by Grok Bot), pinned to one commit each, and may contain errors. Projects without a licence file are explained in prose only, with links to their exact lines on GitHub, never copied. Members are listed only where a GitHub account is linked from their lean.nyc profile, their home page or a Lean NYC talk page. Corrections or take-down requests: open an issue.
